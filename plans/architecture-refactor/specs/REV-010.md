## Objective

Review owns one contract/daemon/client surface with proven active-content
sandboxing, path containment, caps, malformed-input omission, compile-on-read
layers, reviewed-mark reconciliation, and watcher attach-after-miss recovery;
the orphaned CLI canvas writers are gone; and the architecture registry marks
Review `complete` with its six target roots, empty legacy paths, and no new
ledger entry.

## Why this unit exists

REV-001..009 moved the wire, operations, Evidence, CLI nouns, client-runtime,
Web, and mobile onto the target Review catalog and deleted the Feature-era
vocabulary. Leaving Review `migrating` lets a second active-content renderer,
an unsandboxed iframe, a re-added `set-canvas` writer, or a Feature-era
procedure name re-enter without a completed-domain gate. Decision 015 requires
the resilience proof to stay and the historical representations to stay gone;
Decision 012 puts that ratchet in `scripts/architecture/domains.mjs` rather
than prose.

## Behavior to preserve

- Markdown / thesis / section prose render through react-markdown without
  `rehype-raw` (web) or `markdownToHtml` with raw HTML escaped (mobile). A
  `<script>` in prose is text.
- HTML and inline SVG render only through `HtmlView` (`sandbox=""` + `srcDoc`,
  never `src`) on web, and through Files `preview-document.ts`
  (`default-src 'none'`, no scripts, no network) on mobile.
- Parent web CSP stays the backstop against passive subresource loads inside
  `srcdoc`. Do not widen `img-src` / `default-src`.
- Review-set file and section-anchor paths are repo-contained on read
  (`isRepoContained`). Absolute paths and `..` escapes are dropped, not thrown.
- Evidence / Intent documents: traversal, absolute names, dotfiles, and
  symlink escapes are omitted. Over-cap documents are unavailable, not a
  cleared empty state. Checks-only packs remain packs with empty results and
  assets. Half-written `meta.json` stays an empty-checks pack.
- Uncompilable layer patterns are dropped on Project Data read so
  `compileLayers` never throws.
- Reviewed marks keyed to a stale fingerprint are pruned; a refreshed
  fingerprint is kept.
- `review-watch` attaches the `active-review` watcher after the directory
  appears; a miss is swallowed and retried from the companion-root watcher.
- An on-disk `canvas` field of any medium is ignored by `readReviewSet` and
  does not fail the review.
- Feature-era identifiers stay absent under `apps/` and `packages/`
  (`lint-legacy-migrations`).
