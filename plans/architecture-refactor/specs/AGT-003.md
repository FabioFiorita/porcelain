## Objective

Remove the generic Ship/Audit routing surfaces and six retired Audit invariant documents after the
29-row ownership map is complete, while retaining every current owner, proof, security boundary,
and focused procedure. Foundation synchronization and the agent doctor must fail closed if a
retired router, adapter, document path, or active instruction returns.

## Why this unit exists

Decision 014 makes the root delivery loop always-on, keeps Companion explicit-only, and retires
generic routing after unique knowledge has current owners. Decision 015 removes pre-launch
compatibility history while retaining real resilience and safety. AGT-001 landed one owner, proof,
and gate for every 11 Ship and 18 Audit inventory rows; AGT-002 landed the explicit Companion
procedure and its drift gate. The remaining generic skills, adapters, and invariant-doc tree now
compete with those current discovery surfaces.
