# AGT-003 — Delete Ship/Audit and audit docs; strengthen foundation sync

- Status: Draft
- Batch: 8
- Domain: Agent foundations and launch
- Depends on: AGT-001, AGT-002, SUP-003
- Governing decisions: 009, 014, 015
- Primary exemplar: no

## Objective

Delete the retired Ship/Audit skills and Audit documentation only after every indexed responsibility has a live current owner and proof; make foundation synchronization reject their return or a missing replacement.

## Why this unit exists

The architecture's long-term foundation is root instructions plus focused procedures, owner docs/tests, and machine gates. Leaving retired routing skills creates competing authority; deleting them early loses earned invariants.

## Current behavior and evidence

- Ship lives at `.agents/skills/ship/SKILL.md`; Audit lives at `.agents/skills/audit/SKILL.md` and routes to `docs/internals/audit/`.
- The inventory's removal tests require every row to have owner/proof, no Ship/Audit reference, and fresh-agent discovery from root/focused procedures.
- `scripts/sync-agent-foundations.mjs`, `pnpm agents:check`, and `pnpm agents:doctor` are current adapter verification surfaces.

## Scope

- Delete Ship, Audit, and `docs/internals/audit/` only in the final verified removal commit.
- Update `scripts/sync-agent-foundations.mjs`, `scripts/agent-doctor.mjs`, docs indexes, and foundation tests to reject stale adapters/references and verify replacements.

## Non-goals

- Do not delete a current owner doc/test/gate, change product code, or remove audit resilience merely because the Audit skill is deleted.
- Do not remove Companion, web-e2e, mobile, releasing, or their adapters.

## Target ownership and public surface

The sync script owns canonical-to-adapter consistency and rejects any skill/adaptor/reference named Ship or Audit. Foundation ownership map is authoritative and must contain all completed rows. Root and focused current procedures are the only agent discovery surfaces for delivery/audit obligations.

## Behavior to preserve

Preserve Husky/git guard/lint/verify/CI, current owner docs/tests, focused procedures, and all security/recovery/package invariants.

## Legacy behavior to delete

Delete `.agents/skills/ship/`, `.agents/skills/audit/`, their Codex/Claude adapters, and `docs/internals/audit/` only after AGT-001 and AGT-002 gates pass. Delete every stale README/index/reference in the same commit.

## Ordered implementation

1. Run AGT-001 ownership lint and require zero incomplete rows; run AGT-002 language/migration checks.
2. Search tracked files for `.agents/skills/(ship|audit)`, `docs/internals/audit`, `Ship`, and `Audit`; classify each hit as a required current owner reference or deletion target.
3. Update root/focused docs/indexes to point only to current owners, then delete the listed retired directories and adapters in one commit.
4. Extend `sync-agent-foundations.mjs` to fail if Ship/Audit directories, symlinks, skill catalog rows, or textual references remain; fail if the ownership map is absent or has incomplete rows.
5. Extend `agent-doctor.mjs`/fixtures to prove root loop, Companion explicit-only, and focused procedure adapters remain usable after removal.
6. Run `pnpm agents:check`, `pnpm agents:doctor`, `pnpm lint`, `pnpm verify`, and fresh-agent discovery review before commit.

## Tests

- Sync fixtures prove stale Ship/Audit files, adapters, catalog entries, and references fail.
- Doctor proves current hooks/adapters and required discovery surfaces.
- `pnpm agents:check`, `pnpm agents:doctor`, `pnpm lint`, and `pnpm verify` pass.

## Validation and evidence

Attach zero-hit searches after allowed historical plan text is excluded, the complete ownership-map output, foundation command results, and a fresh-agent root/focused-procedure walkthrough.

## Forbidden shortcuts

- Do not delete skills/docs piecemeal or leave redirect/shim files.
- Do not use a broad ignore to hide a stale reference.
- Do not delete before the map/Companion gates pass.

## Completion criteria

No active source, adapter, or docs index references Ship/Audit; sync/doctor enforce the absence and replacements; all full gates and fresh-agent discovery pass.

## Handoff

LCH-002 runs the final canonical/fresh-agent proof and removes the completed migration-ledger entries.
