## Objective

Mobile persists only strict version-1 Remote environments, Settings and
other domains consume them through `apps/mobile/src/features/remote`, and
provider/session health uses REM-003 with no version-3 reader, icon
coercion, or mobile-local error classification.

## Why this unit exists

`lib/daemon/environment.ts` accepts version 3, defaults omitted icons, and
converts `box` to `desktop`. Provider walk, pairing redeem, and session
backoff duplicate REM-003 decisions via `orderedEndpointUrls`,
`toDaemonError`, and local `nextRetryDelay`. Remote has no mobile feature
root.
