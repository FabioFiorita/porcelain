## Objective

`AGENTS.md`, focused procedures, current owner documentation/tests, and a mechanical ownership
lint will expose one exact owner, proof surface, and removal gate for all 11 inventory Ship rows and
all 18 inventory Audit rows, without deleting Ship, Audit, or `docs/internals/audit/`.

## Why this unit exists

Decision 014 makes the delivery loop always-on in the root instructions and retires Ship only after
its unique procedures move to their owners. Audit can retire only after every invariant has a durable
domain/supporting-region owner and an exact proof boundary. The current inventory is still a plan,
so the migration needs one checked index before AGT-002 or AGT-003 can change the remaining agent
foundations.

- The root loop remains one nine-step loop, with proportional proof and an explicit stop before push.
- Objective fixes remain autonomous; scope, architecture, product, dependency, destructive-data,
  and outward-facing choices still escalate under root instructions.
- `pnpm lint`, hooks, branch policy, `pnpm verify`, CI, `agents:check`, and `agents:doctor` keep
  their current responsibilities. `HUSKY=0`, `--no-verify`, and `PORCELAIN_SKIP_VERIFY` remain
  forbidden.
- Browser proof remains Playwright in the browser project against an isolated dev daemon; native
  Electron proof remains a separate Mac-only procedure; mobile proof remains fingerprint-gated.
- Audit safety, recovery, resource, authentication, path, active-content, Git, PTY, packaging, and
  terminal resilience behavior is only indexed and made discoverable here, never weakened.
- Decision 014 keeps product Review publication optional. Companion remains the intentional product
  surface procedure, not an automatic session lifecycle.
