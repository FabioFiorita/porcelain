## Objective

Desktop stays a thin Electron integration layer that owns windows,
preload, one `utilityProcess` child, packaging, local terminal paths,
and this machine's daemon address book; a lint keeps main/preload free
of Web/mobile/domain-feature imports and `child_process` forks; the
child script path and empty argv are proven; `node-pty` and `trash`
stay in the packager asar unpack list.

## Why this unit exists

Leaving Desktop unratcheted lets a second `child_process` daemon, a
Web/feature import into main, or a dropped `node-pty` asar entry land
without a gate. Deleting the address book to satisfy the stale Draft
would reopen completed Remote and break multi-machine pairing. Decision
012 puts the ratchet in a script; ruling A puts the address book in
Desktop on purpose.
