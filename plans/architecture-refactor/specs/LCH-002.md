## Why this unit exists

A completed domain cannot retain a legacy waiver, alias, or stale foundation instruction. Launch needs machine-readable absence proof as well as passing product behavior.

`legacyPaths` field. The deep-import ledger contains no zeroed or completed-caller rows after the
public-index cutover. Architecture, support, CLI, Desktop, and foundation lint gates have no
compatibility exception. Root plus focused procedures are sufficient for a fresh agent to discover
the delivery loop, domain boundaries, proof surface, and optional Companion.

## Legacy behavior to delete

decision/inventory evidence and negative-proof vocabulary where a gate needs it.
