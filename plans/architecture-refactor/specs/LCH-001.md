## Objective

Rehearse a clean launch from explicit isolated version-1 storage and prove that the current protocol
accepts a matching client while refusing a mismatched client with the terminal update-required
outcome, without touching human material.

## Why this unit exists

Decision 015 permits pre-launch compatibility removal but requires intentional defaults, strict v1
formats, and clear protocol mismatch. PDT-001 and REM-004 now own those behaviors; this unit joins
their current fixture and handshake boundaries into one clean-launch rehearsal without turning the
human-only reset authorization into executable code.

## Current behavior and evidence

- `apps/daemon/src/features/project-data/project-data-store.ts` creates a hidden `.porcelain` root,
  current `.gitignore`, and the v1 `project-manifest.json`; it does not call home migration. Its
  fresh-root, legacy-flat-preservation, and no-home-read proofs are in
  `project-data-store.test.ts`.
- `apps/daemon/src/features/projects/projects-recents-store.ts` owns the strict v1
  `projects-recents.json` envelope under the injected daemon userData directory. The daemon entry
  point requires `PORCELAIN_USER_DATA` and initializes this store before serving requests.
- `apps/daemon/src/session/session-handshake.ts` consumes the contracts `sessionHelloFrameSchema`,
  `sessionReadyFrameSchema`, and `sessionMismatchFrameSchema`; `session-gateway.ts` refuses a
  mismatch before watches, subscriptions, or terminal resources exist.
- `apps/daemon/src/features/remote/remote-http.ts` rejects absent, malformed, older, and newer
  request versions with `protocol.update-required` after the route's authentication/rate-limit
  gate and before tRPC/pairing dispatch. `remote-http.test.ts` proves matching dispatch and
  mismatch non-dispatch with the public error envelope.
- `plans/architecture-refactor/authorization/pdt-004-human-reset.md` is filled and says
  `human-owner-only`, `agent runnable: no`, `pnpm wired: no`, `export machinery: no`, and
