## Objective

Viewer keeps virtualized source rendering for files over
`EDITABLE_MAX_LINES`, maps Files `binary` / `too-large` / `not-found`
views to safe unavailable copy, falls back to plain text when
`isTokenizable` is false, stays transport-free under the SUP-001 lint,
and those cases have focused tests at the owning modules. No new
content-model type. No hook-call-site move.

## Why this unit exists

SUP-001 left Viewer I/O call sites in `components/viewer` and ratcheted
them against `lib/trpc` / `lib/daemon` / `@backend`. Leaving the
resilience cases without colocated proof lets an eager editor, a thrown
binary, or a widened tokenize cap land without a gate. Decision 011
puts that proof at `FileContent`, `TextFileView`, `isTokenizable`, and
`VirtualRows` rather than in e2e.

## Behavior to preserve

- Files over `EDITABLE_MAX_LINES` render through `SourceView` /
  `VirtualRows`, not `EditorSource`.
- `FileContent` shows "Binary file · … KB" for `binary`, "File too large
  to preview · … MB" for `too-large`, and "This file no longer exists."
  for `not-found`, and never throws.
- `isTokenizable` is false above 10_000 lines or 2 MiB; `CodeLine` then
  renders plain text.
- `VirtualRows` keeps `overscan: 30` and `ROW_HEIGHT = 20`.
- `html-view` keeps `sandbox=""` + `srcDoc`.
- `optimizeDeps.entries` still includes `**/*.{ts,tsx}`.
- Viewer production files stay free of `lib/trpc`, `lib/daemon`, and
  `@backend/`.
