Porcelain helps you understand and review code produced by agents running in other tools. It gives the review its own space, while your agents keep working where they already run.

<Callout title="In development">
The current repository is a rebuild. The browser foundation works with a local server; the complete review experience and packaged apps are still in progress. No installable release is offered on this site yet.
</Callout>

## A place for the work

The intended workflow starts with a project and a worktree. That selection becomes the context for files, changes, history, comments, and artifacts. Each environment remains authoritative for its own repositories and private data.

## Built beside your tools

Porcelain does not host agents or act as an IDE. Generic terminals, shell actions, and AI commit generation are outside the agreed rebuild. The focus is understanding and reviewing the work.
